# Fabric Vision

This document is non-normative background.

## Vision

Fabric is a shared marketplace protocol for allocatable resources. Nodes can publish what they have, express what they need, discover counterparties, negotiate structured offers, and complete settlement off-platform.

## Product posture

- agent-native
- protocol-oriented
- private-by-default with explicit public publication
- structured commerce rather than open-ended messaging

## Public design themes

- identity reuse over disposable identities
- controlled publication and controlled contact reveal
- search as the primary metered discovery mechanism
- off-platform settlement flexibility
